# SprintLab Private Beta Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 27, 2026

SprintLab is an early private-beta training application.

## Data stored by the app

SprintLab may store the athlete profile, training preferences, planned workouts, readiness responses, completed training logs, performance entries, and app settings.

This beta stores that information locally on the athlete's device. SprintLab does not currently provide an account, cloud synchronization, or cloud backup. Deleting the app or using **Erase all local app data** can permanently remove this information.

## Notifications

If the athlete enables workout reminders, SprintLab uses device notifications to show locally scheduled session reminders. Notification permission can be changed in the device settings.

## External links

The app can open external research and feedback pages. Information submitted on those pages is governed by the provider of that page.

## Health and training information

SprintLab provides training organization and decision support. It does not diagnose injuries, replace a coach, or replace qualified medical care. Athletes should stop and seek appropriate help for severe or concerning symptoms.

## Beta feedback

Beta testers can report problems through the feedback link provided in the app. They should avoid including unnecessary personal or medical information.

## Contact

Questions and beta feedback can be submitted through the in-app feedback link.
