# SprintLab planner test profiles

Use **Settings → Reset app for testing**, complete onboarding with each profile, then open **Plan → Build my week**. The preview should explain every session before it changes the saved week.

## 1. Trained 100/200 m athlete — general preparation

- Main sport: Track & field
- Primary event: 100 m
- Secondary event: 200 m
- Experience: Consistent training
- Schedule: 4 speed-training days
- Access: track, grass/turf, full weight room, cones
- Context: General preparation / offseason
- Limitations: Nothing currently

Expected architecture: **high / low / high / low**. It should include acceleration plus strength, one tempo/capacity day, maximum velocity plus a different strength record, and a technical low day. It should not include special endurance or a meet primer.

## 2. Trained 200/400 m athlete — general preparation

- Main sport: Track & field
- Primary event: 400 m
- Secondary event: 200 m
- Experience: Consistent training
- Schedule: 5 training days and 2 unselected/rest days
- Access: track, grass, hill, full weight room, cones
- Next meet: roughly 24–32 weeks away
- Context: General preparation
- Limitations: Nothing currently

Expected architecture: **high / low / high / low / high** with acceleration plus strength, one extensive-tempo day, maximum velocity plus strength, one technical low day, and controlled speed endurance. The two strength pairings should contain their authored exercises. No duplicate grass-tempo filler and no special endurance.

## 3. Same 400 m athlete — no weight room

Use profile 2, but select home/limited space and no weight room.

Expected change: the same sprint-week roles remain, but high days pair with an Approved no-gym strength/bodyweight record. Limited equipment should change the method, not turn the entire week into recovery.

## 4. Foundation 200 m athlete

- Main sport: Track & field
- Primary event: 200 m
- Experience: Just starting
- Schedule: 5 available days
- Access: track or turf, grass, no gym
- Context: General preparation

Expected change: only two high-output sprint days. The fifth role becomes lower-complexity elastic or bodyweight work instead of a third demanding sprint exposure. Advanced workouts remain excluded.

## 5. Trained 400 m athlete — specific preparation

Use profile 2, but choose specific preparation or place the priority meet inside the corresponding season window.

Expected change: the final quality role may use an Approved introductory special-endurance record. It must still preserve low days between high outputs and must never select Draft or Archived workouts.

## 6. Athlete in competition season

- Track event: 100 m or 200 m
- Experience: Consistent training
- Priority meet: inside the competition window
- Schedule: 3–4 open SprintLab days

Expected change: lower total load, short acceleration/starts quality, a maximum-velocity microdose, recovery/technical work, and meet preparation when allowed by the calendar. Full general-preparation strength should not be attached automatically.

## 7. Athlete near an A-priority meet

- Track event: any supported event
- Priority meet: inside the taper window

Expected change: no full high-output architecture. Only familiar primers, recovery, and a reviewed speed microdose should appear. No novel or high-volume work.

## 8. Coach-managed athlete

- App mode: Follow/log my coach’s plan

Expected result: automatic plan building stays off. SprintLab should preserve manual planning, workout execution, History, and Progress without replacing the coach.

## 9. Football-only athlete

- Main sport: Football
- Do not select Track & field as the primary focus

Expected result: SprintLab explains that track is the first completed automatic-planning pathway. It must not disguise track workouts as a reviewed football plan.

## 10. Sensitivity versus current readiness

- In onboarding, select hamstring sensitivity but no current symptoms.
- Build the week, then complete the pre-workout check-in twice:
  1. normal readiness;
  2. severe soreness, acute pain, or a worse warm-up reassessment.

Expected result: sensitivity alone does not erase the planned week. Current red readiness blocks workout launch. General soreness 5/5 blocks maximal sprinting. Yellow requires a Better / Same / Worse warm-up recheck before proceeding.
