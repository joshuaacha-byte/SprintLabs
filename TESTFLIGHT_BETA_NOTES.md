# SprintLab Beta Testing Notes

## What to test

SprintLab is a speed-training planner and workout log for track athletes and athletes building general linear speed.

Please test:

- onboarding and speed-profile setup;
- the suggested weekly plan and its explanations;
- planned and unplanned workout execution;
- readiness decisions and warm-up reassessment;
- training History and Progress;
- workout reminders;
- Dark and Light appearance;
- the full-data erase control in Settings.

## Important limitations

- Data is currently stored only on the device and is not backed up.
- Track sprinting is the deepest planning pathway.
- Football uses the reviewed 40-yard pathway.
- Other sports currently receive a general linear-speed foundation rather than full sport-specific programming.
- SprintLab does not diagnose injuries or guarantee performance improvements.

Please use the in-app feedback link for bugs, confusing wording, bad layouts, or a suggested week that does not fit the profile you entered.
